Who it's for

Busy owners, not tech people.

Recommend it to

  • Owners of small and growing businesses: shops, salons, trades, service businesses, e-commerce

What to say and not say

Be specific, be honest, never promise money.

Say

  • What the person actually gets: the format, the length and what they leave with
  • Who it's for, and who it isn't for
  • Your own honest experience, if you've taken a course
  • That you earn a commission (see the next section)
  • That they keep control: anything that spends money waits for their approval

Never say

  • Any promise of income, profit, savings or hours saved ("you'll make $10k", "save 20 hours a week")
  • That AI will replace their staff
  • Fake urgency or scarcity ("only 2 seats left", "price goes up tonight") unless we've told you it's true
  • Testimonials or client names we haven't given you
  • That Ward Consulting is part of, partnered with, or endorsed by Anthropic
  • Anything presented as if you are Ward Consulting or Chris

Why so strict: Ward Consulting's own pages promise no results, revenue, savings or income, and US law (FTC) holds both you and us responsible for what you claim.

  • "Do I need to pay for Claude?" Not for the $99 course; the free plan covers almost all of it. The 2-day and 5-day trainings need a paid Claude plan.
  • "Will this save me hours or make me money?" We can't promise that. You leave with AI set up and working on your own tasks; what it does for you depends on your business.
  • "Can I get a refund?" Start Up is fully refundable up to 48 hours before the session. For anything else, ask Ward Consulting directly at hello@wardconsulting.ai.
  • "Is this Anthropic?" No. Ward Consulting AI teaches Claude, which Anthropic makes, but is independent and not affiliated with Anthropic.
  • Anything you're unsure about: send them to hello@wardconsulting.ai rather than guessing.
