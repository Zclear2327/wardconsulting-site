Questions

Straight answers.

  • Do I need to be technical? No. Every step is numbered and written in plain English. If you can install an app and sign in to your email, you can do this.
  • Which email services does it cover? Gmail, Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 work accounts use Claude's email connectors. Personal Outlook.com, Hotmail and Live accounts can't use the Microsoft 365 connector, so the guide shows how to use Claude in Chrome instead.
  • Will Claude delete my email? Not unless you approve it. The Master Prompt tells Claude to ask before any delete, unsubscribe, filter or send, and to archive before deleting. You stay in charge and should review every suggestion.
