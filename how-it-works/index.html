Mission briefing

How it works

Simple, and you stay in charge. No jargon and no big project to start. We begin with the one job you most want off your plate.

1

Talk

A free call. Tell us what takes up your week.

2

Map

We draw your busiest job as a simple workflow: the trigger, each step, who approves, and what "done" looks like.

3

Set up

We set up Grok and your AI helpers in your own account, and show you how to use them. Each helper starts in test mode and only writes drafts.

4

You approve

Anything that spends money or can't be undone waits for your yes, and you can see everything they did. Silence is never a yes.

5

Daily check

A simple daily check shows whether each helper ran, what it made, and what is waiting for your yes.

Ground rules for every helper

  • It says it's AI.
  • It never asks for your passwords. You sign in yourself.
  • It only reads what you point it at.
  • Type PAUSE and it stops.
  • It won't give legal, tax or financial advice. It will suggest you ask a professional.
