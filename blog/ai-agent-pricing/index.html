AI agent pricing

Ward Consulting AI · November 10, 2026

AI agent pricing depends on how many helpers you need, how much setup work comes first and who approves what over time. This guide explains those cost drivers, shows the prices of our own plans as a real example, and lists the questions that make any provider's quote easy to compare. We do not quote other companies' prices here, because those change.

What drives the price of an AI agent

Five things usually decide what a setup costs you each month:

  • How many helpers. One helper for one task costs less to run than a set of helpers across sales, admin and marketing.
  • How much setup comes first. A provider that talks with you, maps your busiest task and checks each draft before it goes live is doing work the price should reflect.
  • Ongoing support. Someone has to read the daily check, update information when your prices or services change and fix whatever drifts.
  • Seats. A solo owner and a team of twenty do not need the same plan.
  • Your own AI account. Many setups need you to pay for your own AI account separately, so ask for the total before you compare.

Ready-made helpers or a custom build

Ready-made helpers cover common work such as inbox sorting, customer questions and drafting posts. A custom build suits a process that is unusual for your trade and needs more mapping. Ask which one you are buying and what a change would cost later. Custom work is often quoted by scope, so ask for the scope in writing before you agree to a number. How to estimate your own monthly total

Price comparisons go wrong when the monthly number is the only number. Build your own total before you compare providers, using the same four lines every time:

  • The plan price you would pay each month, taken from the provider's current page.
  • Any setup charge, noted as a one-time cost or spread across the months you expect to keep the setup.
  • Your own AI account, which is a separate cost for most setups, including ours.
  • Any charge for changes you will need later, such as a new service or a new team member.

When two quotes have similar totals, compare what each one covers and who approves what before you decide. Two quotes that look alike on price can differ a great deal on the approvals that protect your customers and your accounts.

Questions that make quotes comparable

  1. What is the total monthly cost, including every account I must pay for myself?
  2. Is any setup work charged separately, and what does it cover?
  3. Which agents or tasks does the price include, written down?
  4. Who approves a charge or a message before it happens?
  5. What happens to my records and accounts if I cancel?
  6. Do you charge for changes to the setup later?

Check other providers' current plans

Other AI providers and consultants publish their own prices, and those prices change often. Check their current plans directly before you compare. This post does not state their figures, and it does not suggest a price range for them. Read our buying questions alongside any quote you receive.

Where the money goes, and what waits for you

Any spend, any purchase and any change in price waits for your yes. Silence is never a yes. A helper can read, sort and draft, but it does not commit money on its own. Ask any provider to show you exactly where that line sits in their setup.

Ask each provider for the same written summary, in the same order, so the comparison is fair. A short page with the monthly total, the setup charge, the accounts you must pay for yourself, the agents included and the approval rules is enough. If a provider will not put those things in writing, ask why before you go further.

A helper that drafts a quote or a follow-up has done its job only when a person has read it, changed what needs changing and sent it. That is why the approval step is part of the price you are paying for. The monthly fee covers the drafting and the daily check, and the approval step is where you keep control of your money and your customers.

Common questions

How much does AI agent pricing usually cost for a small business?

The cost depends on how many helpers you need, how much setup comes first, the support you want and whether you need seats for a team. Ward Consulting AI lists Starter at $29.99 a month, Pro at $99.99 a month and Elite at $249 a month, and you pay for your own AI account separately.

Is there a free AI agent plan?

Ward Consulting AI lists a free tier at $0 that includes the Email and Inbox Agent and the Workflow Agent, along with free step-by-step training. Paid agents are on the Starter, Pro and Elite plans, and you need your own AI account either way.

Why is it hard to compare AI agent prices?

Providers package setup, support, seats and approvals differently, so the monthly number alone can mislead you. Ask for the total monthly cost, what the setup includes, who approves each action and what happens to your records if you cancel.
