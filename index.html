Your AI agents

6 agents that take work off your plate. The first two are free.

Start with the free Email & Inbox Agent and the free Workflow Agent. Want more? Starter adds 1 paid agent of your choice. Pro and Elite include all 4 paid agents.

  1. 1

    Email & Inbox Agent Free

    Sorts your inbox, flags what needs you today, and drafts replies for you to send.

  2. 2

    Workflow Agent Free

    Maps how your business runs, from first contact to closing the books, puts the right agent on each step, and cuts how often your agents stop to ask permission.

  3. 3

    Customer Questions Agent Starter, Pro & Elite

    Answers the questions customers ask most, from your own information, and hands the tricky ones to you.

  4. 4

    Sales & Follow-Up Agent Starter, Pro & Elite

    Keeps track of leads, drafts follow-ups and quotes, and reminds you so nobody slips through.

  5. 5

    Daily Assistant Starter, Pro & Elite

    Your morning brief: calendar, to-dos, bookings and errands, and an eye on prices or competitors you care about.

  6. 6

    Content & Social Agent Starter, Pro & Elite

    Drafts posts, newsletters and short video scripts in your voice for you to approve.
